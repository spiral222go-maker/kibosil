Kibosil Privacy Policy

Effective: December 1, 2026 · First published: October 2, 2026 · 한국어

This is a translation of the Korean policy. If the two differ, the Korean version governs.

Kibosil (the "app") is made by Jounghyun Yoon (the "operator"). If you don't sign in, none of your personal information goes to the operator. Signing in is your choice. Only for users who sign in, the operator's server (section 2) keeps what's needed for the account and for server game backup. The phone app shows Google AdMob ads, and while ads are shown Google collects the information in section 6.

1. Without signing in — what the app handles and where it stays

When your phone is linked to your PC, the two talk directly over the same Wi-Fi, not through the operator's server. If you installed Tailscale yourself to use it away from home, that connection follows Tailscale's own terms and policy.

2. When you sign in — what the operator's server handles

When you sign in with a passkey or a Google account in Settings → Account, the operator's server handles the following. No passwords are used.

Server game backup only runs after you sign in and turn it on (it's off by default). If a game names other people (such as your opponent), those names are backed up with it — clear the player fields of that game or turn backup off if you don't want that.

The operator uses this information only to provide the sign-in and backup you asked for and to run the service safely. It is not sold or given to anyone else, and it is not used for ads.

3. Where the server is and who runs it for us

  • Server: in the Seoul region of Google Cloud (Republic of Korea). The data in section 2 and the access logs are kept in the Seoul region too. A copy of the server data is made once a day and kept for 7 days.
  • Processor: the operator does not run its own server hardware; Google Cloud runs the server on the operator's behalf.
    ProcessorGoogle Cloud Korea LLC (Google Cloud, Google Cloud Privacy Notice)
    WorkRunning the server and storing the data in section 2 and the access logs (Seoul region)
    PeriodSame as the retention periods in the table in section 2
  • Google sign-in: you sign in to Google directly in Google's sign-in window, and Google sends the operator only the sign-in confirmation. That process follows the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Deleting

  • In the app: Settings → Account → "Delete account".
  • Outside the app: at kibosil.com/delete, confirm it's you the same way as in the app (passkey or Google), then delete.

Deleting removes the account, passkeys and Google sign-in link, and the games backed up to the server right away. Anything left in the daily server copy (section 3) disappears within 7 days. Access logs are deleted after 30 days. Games and records stored on your phone stay (they go away when you uninstall the app).

5. How the information is protected

  • The app and the server talk only over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • The server runs in a space separated from other data on the operator's computer, with only the permissions it needs. Its admin page can't be opened from outside that computer.
  • No passwords are collected (passkeys and Google sign-in). The keys that confirm a sign-in are signed to prevent forgery.
  • Bursts of requests and repeated failed checks are blocked, and access logs are used to watch for unusual access.

6. Ads (Google AdMob)

The banner ad at the bottom of the phone app is shown by Google LLC's AdMob. The PC program has no ads. To choose, show and measure ads, Google's ad component in the app (Google Mobile Ads SDK) sends the following directly to Google.

InformationUsed for
Advertising ID and other device identifiersShowing and personalizing ads, measuring ads, preventing invalid clicks
IP address (approximate location)Showing ads, preventing invalid clicks
App usage related to ads (app launches, ad taps, etc.)Measuring ads, analytics
Diagnostics (app launch time, etc.)Checking errors in the ad component, analytics

The operator doesn't receive this information — only totals such as how many times ads were shown and tapped. How Google uses it is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

To turn off personalized ads, go to your phone's Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads and tap "Delete advertising ID". Ads still appear but are no longer personalized. You can also "Reset advertising ID" to get a new one.

The app has no payments.

7. Downloads from elsewhere

When you analyze on the phone alone ("Without a PC, on this phone"), the app downloads the KataGo network file once from KataGo's official site (katagotraining.org). If you pick a kyu/dan AI, that network comes from the same place. Phones on which KataGo runs very slowly download a lighter older network from KataGo's GitHub release page (github.com). Like any web visit, those sites can see connection information (IP address, etc.), handled under each site's policy. The app sends nothing else about you in the process.

8. Contact

If you send an email through the app's "Report a problem · Contact" or to the address below, the operator receives that email address and content to reply. They are deleted one year after replying. A problem report file, if attached, contains app logs and PC information (operating system, graphics card) but no games.

9. Age

The app's store age rating is 13+. Because Google collects device identifiers for ads (section 6), users under 14 should use the app with a parent's or guardian's consent. The same applies to signing in and server game backup. Ads shown in the app are limited to Teen or lower. Users under 14 should also send contact emails together with a parent or guardian.

10. Your rights

You can see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information at any time. You can see your account number and backed-up games in the app's Settings → Account and delete them as in section 4. Send other requests to the contact in section 11 and they will be handled without delay.
